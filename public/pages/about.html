We are Xerocide from Tampa, Florida. Our name stands for No Genocide. No Suicide. We are a community-driven band writing music to liberate the soul and empower the mind. With Armageddon Records giving us the passage and distribution to push our music forward, we're bringing our own sound to metallic hardcore—rooted in 90s influence from Crowbar, Meshuggah, and Sepultura while carving out something distinctly ours.
